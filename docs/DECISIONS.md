# Nivaran v2: decisions

These win over `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` where they differ. Change a row here
first, then the code. Dates are when the decision was recorded.

| Id | Topic | Decision | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Login | Google plus passwordless email code only. No passwords, no verification link. | 25 Nov 2026 | decided |
| B | Accounts | One user per verified email, linked through `auth0Ids`. | 25 Nov 2026 | decided |
| C | Authorization | Role, level, scope and status from MongoDB, never from the token. | 25 Nov 2026 | decided |
| 1 | Pincode boundaries | Optional. Where a polygon exists (open GeoJSON or drawn by hand) it is stored in MongoDB with a 2dsphere index and a `boundarySource` field so better data can replace it; everywhere else the radius circle of M1-7 is used. Changed 26 Nov 2026 (was: one pilot city first). | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| 2 | Departments | One national category list (codes: `pothole`, `garbage`, `streetlight`, `water_leak`, `drainage`, `other`; changed 26 Nov 2026, see M1-6). Each city admin maps every category to one of their city's departments; every mapping change is audit-logged. "Other" or unmapped goes to a city-admin triage queue. | 25 Nov 2026 | decided |
| 3 | Expected-by (SLA) | Calendar hours from submission, per category per city with a national default. Paused only by the on-hold flag (`slaPausedAt`). A reopened issue gets a fresh deadline. A breach notifies the dept_head, then the city_admin. | 25 Nov 2026 | decided |
| 4 | Photos | Cloudinary, signed by the server (`node:crypto`, no SDK). Max 3 per report, 5 MB each, JPEG/PNG/WebP/HEIC, EXIF stripped, public but unguessable URLs. "After" photos by staff only. Secrets only in `server/.env`. | 25 Nov 2026 | decided |
| 4a | Photos: browser side | Read GPS in the browser before stripping, only to prefill the map pin; compress in the browser to about 1600px. Agreed earlier, to verify. | 25 Nov 2026 | open |
| 5 | Languages | English and Hindi at launch; every UI string behind i18n keys. Regional languages later, Punjabi first. | 25 Nov 2026 | decided |
| 6 | Notifications | In-app and email at launch. No SMS (India DLT registration adds cost and paperwork). | 25 Nov 2026 | decided |
| 7 | Community | Signed-in, verified-email users post only in their own pincode's community. Posts go live immediately; moderation after publishing: report button, auto-hide at about 3 reports pending staff review, per-user rate limit, staff moderation queue. | 25 Nov 2026 | decided |
| O1 | Waitlist emails | HMAC only: `emailHash` = HMAC-SHA-256(`HMAC_SECRET`, lowercased email). Dedupe only; we cannot email the list later. No encryption key. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| O2 | Coverage | **National coverage**, not a pilot city. Every pincode in the directory that has coordinates is served. One `City` per (state, district), created by the import with code `<state code>-<DISTRICT SLUG>` (e.g. `PB-KAPURTHALA`), status `active`; each pincode points at its district city. Replaces "Phagwara pilot" (26 Nov 2026). | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-1 | Ticket ID | `NIV-YYYY-NNNNNN` (year of submission, 6-digit counter per year). Replaces the handoff's `NVN-YYMMDD-NNNNN`. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-2 | Issue status | `submitted, assigned, in_progress, resolved, closed, reopened, rejected`, plus an `slaPausedAt` flag (on hold is a flag, not a status). Replaces the handoff and plan M2 enum. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-3 | Boundary source | Pilot boundaries drawn by hand (geojson.io), stored with `boundarySource: "manual"`. Enum `aikosh, manual, none` so better data can replace them. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-4 | Default theme | Light. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-5 | i18n fallback | `t()` falls back to `en` for a key missing in the user's language. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-7 | Pin check precision | A pin is checked against the pincode's polygon when one exists (`precision: "exact"`), else against a circle around the centroid (`precision: "approx"`) with `radiusM = clamp(max distance from centroid to any directory place + 1500 m, 3000, 25000)`. Replaces the fixed 2 km fallback. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-8 | Directory source | GeoNames `IN.zip` (`data/pincodes.tsv`, CC BY 4.0, credit GeoNames): `localities` = unique place names, `centroid` = mean of the places. The data.gov.in CSV still imports (`--format csv`) but has no coordinates, so its pincodes stay unserved. The `api.postalpincode.in` fallback is out of M1. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-9 | Default departments | A seed gives every city Roads, Sanitation, Electrical, Water, Drainage and the mapping pothole to Roads, garbage to Sanitation, streetlight to Electrical, water_leak to Water, drainage to Drainage, other to triage. City admins change it later (row 2). | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-6 | Categories | `pothole, garbage, streetlight, water_leak, drainage, other`. `other` always goes to the city-admin triage queue. (Stray animals dropped.) | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-10 | SLA national defaults | `SlaPolicy` with `cityId: null` per category, calendar hours (respond / resolve): pothole 24/168, garbage 12/48, streetlight 24/72, water_leak 12/48, drainage 24/72, other 48/168. Seeded by `npm run seed:sla -w server -- --apply` (inserts only what is missing). `expectedBy(submittedAt, hours, pausedMs)` is pure. Ticket year is the IST year. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-11 | Pincode lookup | `GET /api/pincodes/:pin` is public and cacheable for a day; a known unserved pincode is `200` with `served: false` (not an error), an unknown one `404`; both offer the waitlist. A bad pin is `400 INVALID_PINCODE`. Waitlist answers the same `200` for new and repeat sign-ups. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-12 | Upload signing | `POST /api/uploads/sign` needs `issue:create` (signed in, verified). Signed params: `folder nivaran/<NODE_ENV>/reports`, `allowed_formats`, incoming `transformation c_limit,w_1600,h_1600` (drops EXIF). Cloudinary does not enforce `max_file_size` on these signed uploads, so it is not signed: the client pre-checks 5 MB, and on submit `photoCheckService.verifyPhotos` asks the Cloudinary Admin API (fetch + basic auth, 5 s timeout) for each `public_id`: it must exist in our folder, be <= 5242880 bytes and jpg/png/webp/heic, else the asset is deleted and the request is `400 INVALID_PHOTO`; Cloudinary unreachable is `503 UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`. Changed 26 Nov 2026 (was: `bytes` re-check only). EXIF verified 26 Nov 2026 with real uploads of GPS-tagged JPEGs (400 px and 2400 px): the stored original has no EXIF segment, so no strip flag is signed; `public_id` carries the folder prefix and `asset_folder` is set. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-13 | City picker | `GET /api/cities` is searched (`q` prefix, `stateCode`) and cursor-paginated (max 50) instead of a 500 cap, because there are 637 district cities. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M1-14 | Issue submit | `POST /api/issues` needs a UUID `Idempotency-Key` per draft (same key = first issue, `200`). Checks run before one transaction that takes the ticket number (so a failed submit leaves no gap), routes, sets `expectedBy` from `resolveHours` (city policy over national), inserts and audits `issue.created` (ids and category only, never location or text). `title` 5 to 100 chars is required. 10 submits per hour per user. Only the reporter can read an issue in M1. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-1 | Who moves an issue | `utils/issueStateMachine.js`: submitted to assigned (staff, dept_head, city_admin), submitted to rejected (dept_head, city_admin, note required), assigned to in_progress and in_progress to resolved (staff, dept_head, city_admin; resolved needs 1 to 3 after photos), resolved to closed (reporter, or the SLA job after 7 days), resolved to reopened (reporter, note required), reopened to assigned (staff, dept_head, city_admin). No edge: `400 INVALID_TRANSITION`; wrong role: `403`. No status change while on hold. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-2 | Optimistic concurrency | Every issue write sends `version` (the issue's `__v`, returned on every read). The write is one conditional update on `{ _id, __v }` inside the transaction; a mismatch is `409 CONFLICT`. Chosen over a server-only check so a stale screen can't overwrite a newer change. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-3 | Triage assignment | `PATCH .../assign` (city_admin; a dept_head's scope never includes triage) moves a triage issue to a department of its own city and sets it `assigned` in the same write. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-4 | On hold | dept_head only, for open issues. `slaPausedAt` + `holdReason`; resume shifts `expectedBy` and `atRiskAt` by the time held. Audited (`issue.hold`, `issue.resume`), reporter notified. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-5 | SLA job | In process, `SLA_JOB_INTERVAL_MS` (default 5 min, 0 = off, never in tests), one instance (ADR 0001). `atRiskAt` is stored (75% point) so every step is index-backed. Breach: dept_head notified (a triage issue: city_admin at once); 24 h later the city_admin; each step claimed by setting `breachNotified*At` in the same transaction as the notifications. SLA uses `resolveHours` only; `respondHours` is not tracked yet. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-6 | Notifications | In-app only in M2 (email later, decision 6). Rows hold `type`, `ticketId`, `status`, never free text; TTL 90 days. Reporter: on every staff change; staff: SLA breach and escalation. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-7 | Public stats | `GET /api/stats`: total, resolved (resolved + closed), median hours submit to resolved (`$median`, MongoDB 7+), open by category. In-memory cache 5 min, `Cache-Control: public, max-age=300`. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-8 | Staff reads | Staff work from the queue rows (description, location, photos); `GET /api/issues/:ticketId` stays reporter-only in M2. state_officer and super_admin can read the queue but hold no write permission. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-9 | Photo URLs and the M2 screens | Every photo in an issue answer carries `url` (Cloudinary public delivery URL, built on the server from `CLOUDINARY_CLOUD_NAME`, `null` when unset), and the reporter's ticket returns its report `photos`, so the client needs no cloud name. Staff actions are panels inside the issue drawer, not a dialog on top (UI.md 6). The drawer reads its row from the cached queue by `ticketId`; every write sends `version`, a 409 refetches. Status changes are never optimistic. The shared `Table` header is no longer sticky (inside the overflow wrapper it covered the first rows). | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M2-10 | dept_head and triage | A dept_head sees and assigns their own city's triage issues (queue list and `PATCH .../assign` scope: own department OR own city with `queue: triage`); status, hold and resume stay department-only. `GET /api/departments` also serves `issue:assign` holders without `user:promote` (dept_head), own city only (another city is 404); the admin path (`user:promote`) is unchanged. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| D-1 | Docker (ADR 0004) | API and web images from the repo root, compose with an optional `local-db` MongoDB replica set, `npm run docker:smoke`, CI `docker` job (Buildx + Actions cache, no push, smoke with dummy env and local MongoDB). Production env checks unchanged; secrets only at run time. Supersedes ADR 0001's "no Docker". | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M3-1 | Community content | Plain text posts (title 5-100, body 10-2000) and comments (1-1000), no Markdown, so no `marked`/`sanitize-html`: React escapes text. Post in your `defaultPincode` only (it must have a city, which scopes moderators); read any pincode's feed when signed in. Two comment levels. `commentCount` = published comments, moved in the same transaction. Limits: 5 posts a day, 30 comments and 30 flags an hour per user. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M3-2 | Flags and moderation | One flag per user per item (unique index; a repeat is a silent `200`). The 3rd flag hides the item in the same transaction, audited as system `community-flags`. Moderators (`post:moderate`, own city; super_admin all) hide or delete with a reason, restore without one; restore resets `flagCount` to 0 and keeps the old flags. Authors may delete their own. Audit rows hold status and flag count, never the text. Pure table: `utils/postStateMachine.js`. | 26 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M3-3 | Help chat | `POST /api/chat`, nothing stored. Ticket ids and "my complaints" are answered from MongoDB (reporter's own, or the staff scope), never by the model; another person's ticket answers like a missing one. Other text goes to Gemini (`@google/genai`, `GEMINI_MODEL` default `gemini-2.5-flash`, 300 output tokens, 8 s timeout) after emails and phone numbers are redacted; no key or any failure is `kind: "fallback"`. 20 messages per user per 10 minutes. | 27 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M3-4 | Community screens | `/community` (signed in) shows the home pincode's feed, or `?pincode=` for any other (read only there); no home pincode = an empty state linking to the profile. The pincode is drawn as the six-box stamp (the page's one bold element, UI.md §1). Cards reveal with the list stagger (30 ms, first 8). `/community/:id`: post, two-level thread, optimistic comments with rollback (UI.md §7), Report (reason select) for others' content, Delete behind an AlertDialog for your own. Composer is a ResponsiveDialog, pessimistic. | 27 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M3-5 | Moderation and chat screens | `/moderation` (`post:moderate`, linked from the account menu): Posts / Comments toggle kept in `?type=`, cards with the report count, Restore (optimistic: the row leaves at once, comes back with a toast on failure) and Delete behind an AlertDialog that needs a 3+ character reason. The chat button opens the conversation for signed-in users (a sign-in step otherwise, closing the chat sheet first): two suggestion buttons, Enter sends, answers in a `role="log"`, ticket answers as cards (linked only for the asker's own list), general answers marked "General guidance, not an official answer.", 429 and network failures in our own words. The conversation lives in memory and clears when the sheet closes. | 27 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M3-6 | City settings | City admins (`user:promote`, own city) and super_admin (any city, picked first) set, per category, the department (or triage; `other` is always triage) and the city's respond/fix hours, which then win over the national default (decision 3). One `PUT` per category upserts `CategoryMapping` and the city `SlaPolicy` and audits `city.category_update` in the same transaction. New reports only; filed issues keep their `expectedBy`. Screen `/admin/city-settings`, one card per category, pessimistic save. | 27 Nov 2026 | decided |
| M3-7 | Hindi | Decision 5 done for the product screens: `client/src/i18n/hi.json` + `hi/*.json` hold 591 strings (everyday Hindi, "आप", common loanwords such as पिनकोड and टिकट in Devanagari, ticket IDs and digits unchanged). The developer-only style guide stays English through the fallback. A test checks every Hindi key exists in English with the same `{placeholders}`. The language comes from the signed-in user's `preferredLanguage`, or for visitors from this browser (`nivaran-language` in localStorage, English if storage is blocked). `AppLayout` sets it before rendering, sets `<html lang>` (Devanagari line heights in `globals.css`) and remounts the frame on a change. The landing footer switch saves to the profile when signed in. Not yet: dates and numbers still use the English formats; Zod messages built at import time stay English. | 27 Nov 2026 | decided |
